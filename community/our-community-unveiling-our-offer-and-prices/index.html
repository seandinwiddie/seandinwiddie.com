Our Community: Unveiling Our Offer and Prices

Call or email

This page sets out the community’s offer and prices, from Sean Paul Payne Dinwiddie’s Software Firm/Practice/Consults. Administrators find the course, recordings and manuals here, discuss the work with other members, and put what they learn to work in their own offices. The offers and prices follow.

What We Bring to the Table

The online country club for administrators offers three tiers:

1. Beta Access for Early Signups

For early adopters and pioneers, we’re extending an exclusive invitation. Get a taste of our community and explore the wealth of knowledge and resources it holds. It’s a chance to experience firsthand the transformative power of our platform.

2. General Access: $1,000 a month

General Access is the full membership. This tier grants you access to a wide range of resources, discussions, and tools that will help you streamline your administration tasks and achieve peak efficiency.

3. Inner Circle: $4,000 a month

The Inner Circle is for members who want the closest mentorship. Dive deep into advanced topics, receive personalized guidance, and connect with a select group of administrators who are determined to excel. This tier is your ticket to the forefront of administration excellence.

Memberships and the team

Memberships are for administrators who keep learning after handover. Webmasters on the Sean Dinwiddie’s Webmastery team belong through the quality of their work, never by buying in: joining asks for quality work, and no fee, course or purchase stands in the way. Whether you’re a solo administrator or part of a larger office, there’s a place for you in our community.

Course Content

The course content is the community’s library. It includes a mixture of community recordings, member demonstrations, user guides, and proprietary manuals. It serves every level of practice, from beginners to seasoned administrators, and follows the course’s three subjects: user stories, behavior-driven development and functional reactive programming.

Important Note

Membership prices can change; a fee written into a signed scope does not. We continuously work to enhance the value we provide to our members and may adjust our pricing to reflect these improvements.

Join Our Community

Administration keeps changing, and the lessons are revised as it does. A seasoned administrator and a newcomer read the same lessons, and each takes from them as much as their practice is ready for.

What the community and the team bring to a member’s work in software development and consulting:

  • DFY, DWY, Coaching: +4k/m Retainer Services. This is the Inner Circle’s mentorship, apart from any client retainer: “Done for You” (DFY), “Done with You” (DWY) and personalized coaching. The member chooses how: the work done for them, done with them, or coached while they do it themselves.
  • Agency Services. The agency services help a member turn first inquiries into scoped work. This includes the path from a first contact to an inquiry, through ads, social media, webpages/sites, email, video sales, and networking. Our agency services also cover each phase of a serious inquiry: discovery, scoping, maintaining scope, hiring contractors, and delivering work held to one standard.
  • The Team’s Expertise. The Sean Dinwiddie’s Webmastery team holds every deliverable to one standard. This encompasses design, development, engineering, architecture, DevOps, and security. We also provide onboarding, style guides, and technical documentation for keeping software maintained, focusing on three top-level subjects: User Stories, Behavior-Driven Development (BDD), and Functional Reactive Programming (FRP). The stack follows the project: many brochure sites run on WordPress, set to update themselves, while custom apps follow the chain the course teaches, from React views over Redux Toolkit to a Haskell (Servant) API on Postgres.
  • Active Documentation. The courses cover software development and consulting, from the first user story to the review before launch, and are kept current as the practice improves.
  • Community. The online country club for administrators, where members bring their questions and learn from one another’s work. We offer beta access for early signups, as well as General and Inner Circle memberships for an even deeper level of engagement.

How webmasters on the team are paid

Webmasters on the Sean Dinwiddie’s Webmastery team work as subcontractors from their own practice and keep 70% of the fee on their client work. That 70% never comes from recruiting, memberships or the community’s growth, and Joining the team sets out the terms.

Membership Prices and the Split

Please note that membership prices can change. The terms for webmasters on client work don’t move with them: the split is 70/30 on every job, and each job’s written subcontract names the fee.

At Sean Paul Payne Dinwiddie – Software Firm/Practice/Consults, the community is held to the same standard as the client work.
