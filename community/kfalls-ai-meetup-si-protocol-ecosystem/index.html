Where things break: the joins

A booking form posts to a calendar. A script reads the price list each morning. An assistant orders from a coffee cart.

Each is a join, where something crosses from one side to the other. When nothing checks what crosses, the customer finds the fault first.

Lesson: Module 6: AI protocol ecosystem

Ask which joins the room’s businesses run on, then what checks what crosses each one today. A join with nothing to name in that last part is the next one to give a contract.

SI: what federal agencies now call AI

Since September 29, 2026, federal agencies write Super Intelligence, SI, in their own documents and communications, where they wrote artificial intelligence, AI.

Executive Order 14434 changes the name, not the law: it defines SI by the statutory definition of AI, so the two cover the same systems. Proposed language for a federal definition is due to the President within 60 days.

In research, “superintelligence” means something else. Here, SI is only the new name.

Sources: the order and its fact sheet · The course’s modules keep “AI” in their names: Module 6

Say it once, plainly, and move on. The order is titled “Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence.” It binds the executive branch’s departments and agencies from now on, and leaves statutes, regulations and earlier documents as they are. For its purposes SI means what the statutory definition of AI, 15 U.S.C. 9401(3), covers, and the legal summaries agree it changes no law. No claim about what the systems can do rides on the name, and from here on the talk says SI.

One join, one agreement

Each join an SI system crosses has an open agreement of its own.

MCP joins an app to tools, A2A one agent to another, OpenAPI a client to an API.

Ask any new protocol which two things it joins, what crosses and what checks it.

Lesson: The AI protocol map

MCP is the Model Context Protocol; the lessons teach its 2026-07-28 revision, which dropped the handshake older tutorials teach. A2A is the Agent2Agent protocol, at 1.0. AGENTS.md and Agent Skills are file formats, not wire protocols, and llms.txt is a proposal.

Who decides

MCP sorts what a server offers by who decides when it’s used.

The model calls tools. The app attaches resources. A person picks prompts.

And a person should always be able to deny a tool call.

Model-controlled never means unsupervised. Sort a few with the room: today’s menu is a resource, adding an item is a tool, the week’s special is a prompt, and a refund is a tool a person approves every time it’s called.

Points, traits and distance

A coffee cart’s answers vary on three traits, the space’s axes: brief or detailed, formal or casual, cautious or bold.

A point takes one level of each: 011 is brief, casual and bold. Three traits make eight points.

Distance is how many traits differ.

Lesson: Geometric reasoning as data

A two-level full factorial, in a statistician’s words; Hamming distance, in a coder’s. Nothing derived is stored: the points and distances come from the declared traits every time, so they can’t drift. For owners: the voice is chosen on purpose, a point in the design, and measured there.

Change one fact, and the answer must flip

A pair is two fact sheets one fact apart, in invented words.

Flip a fact on the proof’s path, and the answer must move. Flip a distractor, and it must not.

Score the pair, not the item: always answering yes gets every yes item right, and not one pair whose answer flips.

A guess that ignores the flip gets no pair right.

Invented words keep what a model already knows about the world from standing in for the premises. Contrast sets make this move by hand; here a generator makes it in code, and an oracle labels both sides, so no answer is typed by hand.

Hold the facts, move the voice

A matched family is one fact sheet answered at every point.

The facts and the answer stay locked; only the voice moves. So any difference between two answers belongs to the traits that differ.

For the cart, the tone can change and the oat milk answer can’t.

Lesson: Geometric reasoning as data

Every field of an answer is declared as invariant or expressive, and a field nobody declared is a fault, never a default. The validator reports every fault at once, so one repair round fixes them all.

The model proposes; typed code decides

In tandem, a harness runs beside the model on every request.

The model proposes a time and a reply. Typed code decides what’s on offer, what goes out and what gets held.

The model’s options and the checker’s come from one list.

Lesson: The tandem harness

Research names the pairing LLM-Modulo: a language model can’t verify its own plans, so external checkers do. Here the one offer list feeds both the schema that narrows what the model can write and the checker that decides what the harness accepts, and a property test keeps them agreed.

Every failure is refused, never passed through

  • A reply goes out exactly as written, or not at all.
  • A rejected answer goes back with every reason, until one passes or the tries run out.
  • Out of tries or out of time, the customer hears the salon’s own line, and the failure stays in the records.

Lesson: The tandem harness

Fail closed. A fallback has no field for a reply or a hold, so it can’t carry one. Internal failures never reach the customer, and every reply that went out traces back through the records to what made it.

Server and client in tandem

A booking crosses the wire in two rounds, with no session kept between them.

The server asks which time, and the app asks the customer. The retry carries the booking, the answer and a signed state that works once.

The customer answers one question, and a time the server never offered can’t be booked.

Lesson: Server and client in tandem

The shape is MCP’s multi round-trip request. The state is signed, short-lived and spent once, and a time never offered is refused before anything is booked. On the client, every reply decodes strictly or not at all.

Extend, don’t fork

A harness grows at documented points; the model and the core loop stay fixed.

One question sorts every addition: is it judgment, or must it happen every time?

Judgment goes where the model reads it. Every time goes to code.

Lesson: Extending the harness

An instruction in a skill or a CLAUDE.md is a request; a hook that blocks is enforcement. The same rule holds for the harness shipped in an app, which grows by composition, and every extension carries a scenario. For owners: an extension rides along at the next update, where a patched core has to be patched again by hand.
