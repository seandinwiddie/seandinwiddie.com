This guide sets out how the Sean Dinwiddie’s Webmastery team builds custom software, lesson by lesson.

The team’s work runs from the people who will use the result to the review before launch, and the course follows the same path, one link at a time.

Whether you keep learning after handover or build for owners on the Sean Dinwiddie’s Webmastery team, you learn the craft from the same lessons.

The lessons teach three parts of that work, in order: user stories, behavior-driven development and functional reactive programming.

The lessons run in teaching order, each building on the one before. Every level reads the same lessons, and a more practiced reader takes more from each.

The lessons follow the course outline, and Sean’s Functional Programming Lectures teach the functional TypeScript and Redux foundation beneath them, beginning with The Simplest FP TypeScript Hello World.

About us

Sean Dinwiddie’s Webmastery is a local webmastery agency working across Jefferson State — Klamath Falls, Redding, and the towns in between. Shops, restaurants, food trucks, salons, kiosks, contractors, clinics. Whether that is one person or fifty.

We build the site, get you found by the people searching nearby, and hand it over so you can run it yourself. When something breaks, you call someone who already knows your site.
