Your website should bring in calls, orders, and bookings — not just sit there looking tidy.

We design sites for the businesses around Klamath Falls and Redding: shops, trucks, salons, kiosks, contractors, clinics.

Why most small-business sites bring in nothing

They were built as a brochure. Someone lands on the page, can't find your hours or your phone number, and goes back to the search results.

We start from the one thing you want more of — a phone call, an online order, a booked slot — and build the page around getting it.

What you get

One job per page. Hours, prices, the menu, where to park, and the button that books you. Near the top, not three clicks down.

Built for the phone first. Most people find you while they're already out. The site has to work one-handed, on a weak signal.

Loads fast. Compressed images and lean pages, so nobody gives up waiting and calls the place down the street instead.

Yours to change. New hours, new prices, a new photo — you get shown how, and it's written down. Whether that's one person or fifty, somebody there can keep it current.

Our process

1. Who you serve, what you want more of, and what the other places nearby are doing.

2. What goes on which page, the path to booking or ordering, and what counts as it working.

3. Rough layouts first, so changes are cheap. Then the finished thing.

4. Launch, then watch real people use it and fix what trips them up.
