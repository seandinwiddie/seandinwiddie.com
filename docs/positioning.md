# Sean Dinwiddie's Webmastery: positioning

Sean Dinwiddie's Webmastery (Webmastery for short) is a network of independent webmasters serving Jefferson State (Klamath Falls, Redding, and the towns in between) under one brand. Founded by Sean Paul Payne Dinwiddie.

## How it works

Independent webmasters work under the Webmastery brand, the way independent agents work under an insurance agency. Each job goes to the webmaster whose level fits it:

| Level | Typical work |
|---|---|
| Junior | A first site for a food truck, kiosk, salon or shop; Google Business Profile; updates; local search |
| Mid | Online ordering, bookings, ads and integrations for restaurants, contractors and clinics |
| Senior | Fractional CTO for local enterprise and government offices: technology strategy, vendors, security, accessibility |

## Audiences

Site copy speaks to one of two readers.

### 1. People who run something local

A kiosk, a shop, a restaurant, a food truck, a salon, a contracting business, a clinic, a local company, a city or county office. Often it's one person wearing several hats: they work for the county, own a local business and run a kiosk. The copy speaks to that one reader at whatever scale they operate.

They want:

- a local webmaster who already knows their site when something breaks
- to be found by people searching nearby
- a site they run themselves after handover
- prices they see up front

Government offices buy through procurement, so copy for them speaks to requirements, deadlines and contracts.

### 2. Webmasters

Independent junior, mid and senior webmasters who work under the brand.

They get:

- the Webmastery brand and local clients
- standard packages with published prices
- a path from junior to mid to senior

## Promises

- Published prices, with nothing hidden from the customer.
- Handover: clients run their own sites, with no lock-in.
- A real ladder from junior to senior for webmasters.

## Government opening

ADA Title II requires state and local government websites and apps to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. The compliance dates are April 26, 2027 for public entities serving 50,000 or more people (Redding) and April 26, 2028 for smaller entities and special districts (Klamath Falls). Accessibility compliance is how the senior level starts working with government offices. Source: [DOJ interim final rule of April 20, 2026](https://www.jacksonlewis.com/insights/doj-extends-public-entities-compliance-deadline-ada-related-website-accessibility-hhss-may-2026-deadline-still-looms).

## Copy review

Each page is judged against the reader it serves:

- **Client pages:** a local operator sees their kind of business, what they get, the price and the next step.
- **Webmaster pages:** a webmaster sees what they get, how the ladder works and how to join.
