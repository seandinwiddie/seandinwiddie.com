# Packages: proposal for Sean's approval

Status: **proposed by the senior CTO agency, awaiting Sean's approval.** No amount below goes on the site until Sean approves it. The prices page stays the cornerstone, and Sean owns it; every figure here is a share of its two published figures, $6,000 a month and $120,000 a year.

## The model: one rate, bought in shares

- **The unit is the monthly engagement at $6,000:** a month of webmaster work on a written scope. It is a scope, never a count of hours or days.
- **A share** is a defined portion of that engagement at the same rate: a tenth, a quarter, a half or a full share, and larger jobs in whole shares. Bought once, a share is a flat fee for a defined job. Held each month, it is a service and maintenance contract. The whole engagement held each month is the retainer.
- **The annual engagement at $120,000 is twenty months of work carried across twelve** (20 × $6,000): the monthly engagement's own scope every month (12 × $6,000 = $72,000), four months of fractional CTO leadership ($24,000) and four months of an ADA Title II / WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility program ($24,000). It is a larger scope at the same rate, never 12 × the month and never a discount.
- Fees rise in exact proportion to the work, so more work costs more, nothing is discounted, and a kiosk and a county office pay the same rate.
- "Share" is a share of an engagement's work, never ownership, equity or an investment. Shares name the size of the job, never the worth of the client. The site never breaks a share into days, weeks or hours.
- **Shared engagements (optional):** several owners can share one monthly engagement, each with their own written scope and named webmaster, at the same rate.

1. **The Tenth Share ($600):** one job with a known end. Listings matched for one location (Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Facebook); one repair (a broken form, an expired certificate, a page that won't load); one hours, menu or price change carried across the site and listings; one team training session with written notes. 2. **The Quarter Share ($1,500):** one page designed, built and handed over (a food truck's menu, an event page, a clinic's booking page); one automation; one conversion leak found, fixed and measured; a small site's search fix list. 3. **The Half Share ($3,000):** an accessibility review against WCAG 2.1 AA for the templates the scope names (findings in priority order, a remediation plan priced in shares, a draft accessibility statement); a search foundation for one location; a refresh of an existing site. The entry point for a public office. Excludes remediation, document fixes and legal advice.
4. **The Full Share ($6,000), and whole shares:** a website designed, built and handed over (hours, menu or prices, map, tap-to-call, booking link, structured data, listings); remediation of the templates a review names; the first share of a custom app, ending in screens you can click through and a written fee for the rest. Two shares ($12,000) for a site that takes orders, bookings or payments, or covers several locations; custom builds in three to twelve shares ($18,000 to $72,000), each approved before it starts.
5. **The Care Contract ($300 or $600 a month):** after handover, a webmaster who knows the site; hosting managed in the owner's own account; certificates, updates, backups, uptime watch, security patching and small fixes. $600 for a site that takes orders, bookings or payments, or that covers automations too. Prices `/automation/`'s "Monthly maintenance". Whether Care is optional is Sean's decision.
6. **The Standing Share ($1,500 or $3,000 a month):** ongoing local search, ads management at a flat fee (never a percentage of spend; the owner pays the ad platform directly under a written ceiling), or conversion work; at the half share, all three together.
7. **The Monthly Engagement ($6,000 a month):** a site and systems that need work every month. A sample composition: Care (a tenth, $600), local search (a quarter, $1,500), conversion or service work (a quarter, $1,500), and the monthly work list and planning call (two-fifths, $2,400), with automation upkeep and a written monthly note. New builds beyond the work list, ad spend and third-party fees are outside it; fractional CTO leadership and the accessibility program belong to the year.
8. **The Year Share ($24,000 a year, or $2,000 a month):** for a public office or firm that needs part of the year's scope: an accessibility review and remediation plan, priority remediation within four shares, the accessibility statement and reporting process, one staff training session, quarterly check-ins and technology advice.
9. **The Annual Engagement ($120,000 a year, suggested at $30,000 a quarter):** the monthly engagement every month, plus fractional CTO leadership (an annual technology plan, budget input, vendor and contract review, procurement support, a security review, briefings for a council or board) and an accessibility program (a review of sites, apps and key documents, remediation of the office's own web content within scope, an Accessibility Conformance Report, the statement and reporting process, staff training, quarterly conformance notes).

The community's memberships (General Access at $1,000 a month and the Inner Circle at $4,000 a month) sit beside the ladder, not on it: they are for administrators who keep learning after handover, and appear only on Training and the community pages.

## Decisions and guardrails

Decisions only Sean can make:

- The model: one rate sold in shares, the year as twenty months of work, the denominations (tenth, quarter, half, full; a twentieth for Care; fifths of the year), and whether the tenth is the smallest one-time fee.
- Names: "the monthly engagement" and "the annual engagement", or "the Month" and "the Year".
- Delivery: shares are not time; each scope names its own delivery date.
- Retainer terms: the first term, notice, whether unused work rolls over, billing in advance, termination for convenience.
- Care and support: what support covers, response times, and whether an owner can decline Care.
- Ownership (recommended: the owner holds the domain, hosting and accounts; code and content are the owner's once paid; the webmaster's pre-existing tools are licensed, not transferred).
- Third-party costs (recommended: billed to the owner directly by each provider, so every fee stays a share).
- Payment schedules, how far the free first conversation and automation audit go, who holds the fractional CTO role, travel between Klamath Falls and Redding.
- Commission: how it's calculated from shares without any incentive to upsell; every out-of-scope quote needs the client's approval.
- Procurement: registration (OregonBuys, Cal eProcure), insurance, W-9 and licenses. Oregon's small procurement goes up to $25,000 (ORS 279B.065), so the $24,000 year share fits under it; $25,000 to $250,000 needs three quotes (ORS 279B.070), where the $120,000 year falls. Splitting a procurement to stay under a threshold is prohibited, so the year share is never pitched that way.

Legal cautions:

- Accessibility work is technical conformance work against WCAG 2.1 AA, reported as status on a stated date. Never "ADA compliant", "certified", "guaranteed", "lawsuit-proof" or "100%". The office's own counsel decides legal questions. No accessibility overlays (the FTC's April 2025 order against accessiBe). An Accessibility Conformance Report is a self-report, not a certification.
- No ranking guarantees; reviews never bought or rewarded; ads never paid by percentage of spend. The ADA Title II dates are the only urgency, stated plainly.
