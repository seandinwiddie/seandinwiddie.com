# Sean Dinwiddie's Webmastery

This repo is the site for **Sean Dinwiddie's Webmastery** (short form: Webmastery). Positioning and audiences:

@docs/positioning.md

## Writing rules

These apply to every doc and all site copy.

- **Present tense, forward-facing.** Describe Webmastery as it operates: "Independent webmasters work under the Webmastery brand." Never "we plan to", "soon", "eventually", "we're building" or "coming soon".
- **No invented specifics.** Present tense describes the model; it never invents facts. No counts of webmasters or clients, named clients, testimonials, credentials or results unless they are real.
