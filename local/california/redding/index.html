A direct, independent partner

You work directly with Sean from the first conversation through delivery and support. The process starts with your business goal, followed by a clear scope and a practical implementation plan.

How the work moves

  1. Clarify the goal. Review the current site or workflow, the audience it serves, and the specific outcome the project should support.
  2. Define the scope. Separate required work from optional improvements, identify dependencies, and agree on the deliverables before implementation.
  3. Build and review. Implement the agreed work, check it on relevant screen sizes, and review the result against the original goal.
  4. Hand off clearly. Document the parts the organization needs to operate, then identify any maintenance or support that remains useful.
