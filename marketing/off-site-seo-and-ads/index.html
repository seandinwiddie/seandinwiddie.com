Off-site SEO and paid ads

Off-site is everything that happens away from your website: who links to you, who mentions you, where your name and hours show up, and what you pay to appear at the top.

What we do

Get your name onto other sites. The chamber, the local paper, the tourism board, your suppliers, the event you sponsor, the "best breakfast in town" roundup. Google counts who vouches for you, and so do the people reading it.

Make your details match everywhere. Name, address, phone, hours — identical on Google, Apple Maps, Yelp, Facebook, and the directories. Two versions of your hours costs you customers who show up to a locked door.

Run Google Ads. For showing up at the top the week you need it: a new location, a slow season, a competitor opening across the street. Tight targeting, an honest number for what a call is worth to you, and a hard ceiling on spend.

Run social ads. Facebook and Instagram are better at reminding people you exist than at catching them mid-search. We'll tell you which one your money belongs in, and it isn't always ours to run.

Handle reviews. Asking for them properly, answering the bad ones without making it worse, and never buying any.

Our process

1. What the other places nearby rank for, who links to them, and what one customer is actually worth to you.

2. Tracking set up first, so you can tell an ad that works from one that just spends.

3. Ads live, listings straightened out, and outreach to the sites worth being on.

4. A monthly note: what came in, what it cost, what we're changing.

What we won't do

Buy links. Ads are rented traffic and links are earned — bought ones get you penalized, and the penalty outlasts whoever sold them to you.
